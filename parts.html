Controller spotlight

ESP32-S3

The Lonely Binary ESP32-S3 N16R8 Gold Edition with external IPEX antenna runs the scoreboard. It drives the RGB matrix over HUB75E, reads the three buttons, and keeps the iPhone controller in sync over Bluetooth LE.
